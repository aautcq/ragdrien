# Adrien Autricque Portfolio

## About

I'm **Adrien Autricque**, a 35-year-old French developer, passionate about the web. I currently work at **Exotec** as a senior front-end developer, focusing on creating modern and evolving applications.

I enjoy working with cutting-edge technologies such as Vue.js, Nuxt, or NestJS. You can explore [my projects](https://me.aautcq.dev/projects) or check out [my skills](https://me.aautcq.dev/skills) for more details.

### Beyond the Code

Aside from coding, I'm deeply interested in music, photography, and cinema. I recently composed the soundtrack for the video game [Playground](https://playground.chapo-s-games.com/), which you can listen to on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4K0q5OL5MYF791XJLW5IVk?si=6KyYkRabQravnUbQe1J5RA) and [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/aautcq). Some of my photographs are shared on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/adrienautcq), and you can still find my old YouTube channel, [Pomone TV](https://www.youtube.com/@pomonetv7022), where I used to talk about films I like.

Before becoming a developer, I completed a PhD in physics of plasmas, where I modeled interactions between solid surfaces and plasma fusion. My work was published in peer-reviewed journals (available on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/adrien-autricque)) and summarized in my [thesis](https://www.theses.fr/2018AIXM0315).

### Contact

I currently live in Lille, France. Feel free to contact me if you're in the area — I'm always happy to meet new people, whether it's for taking photos, coding together, or just having a chat.
